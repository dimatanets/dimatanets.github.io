Order management
UX audit

Role

UX Designer

Date

2025

Company

HS-Soft

Context

WaWiAssist is HS-Soft's cloud-based order management system for bakeries. It covers the daily back-office work: orders from delivery customers, production planning, picking and invoicing.

Goal

Conduct a UX audit of the product and find ways to reduce time on task in its most business-critical workflows.

Outcome

Using analytics, heatmaps, session recordings and stakeholder interviews, I found three recurring problems: hidden navigation, a hard-to-find key filter and search results lost behind active filters. I handed over redesign proposals with success metrics and a PostHog setup to measure each change after release.
