Restaurant QR menu platform

Role

Product Designer

Date

2024

Client

WizMenu

Context

WizMenu is a product for restaurants, built around creating and managing digital QR menus. The client came to me with a raw idea and no existing product.

Goal

Design the whole product from scratch: a complete ecosystem connecting an admin panel, a waiter app and a guest menu that any restaurant could adapt to its own brand.

Outcome

After competitor research, interviews with restaurant owners and several wireframe iterations, I delivered a development-ready design system, UI for every module and reusable white-label menu templates. The product is now in beta.
